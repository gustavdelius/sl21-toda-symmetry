## Outcome

A concrete single interaction-insertion map can now be specified, with a balanced symmetric normalization. The first-order mixed pair is explicitly constructed, including its ordinary principal-value dressing, its single-current contact terms, and its full pair contact. Second-order pair forests and their descendants have explicit coefficients. The map is applied once to an already composed Gaussian cluster; it is not inserted into each binary multiplication.

The critical new point is that balanced pair matching alone is insufficient. A spatial derivative and the two-dimensional Cauchy double pole differ by a delta function. Those unary source/current contacts generate nonzero symmetric density terms. We compute and retain them. Setting all symmetric contacts to zero would give the wrong map.

The first one-barred Serre descent also passes at first order in the Yukawa couplings. The full mass-order-two descent remains open: the once-applied forest map has not yet been shown to give equal values on every canonically equal composite. Nothing here identifies a Hilbert-space symmetry algebra or proves an all-order interacting product.

## 1. Conventions and the domain of the proposed map

Use the primary vectors

`a0=(ib/sqrt(2),b/sqrt(2),i)`, `a2=(ib/sqrt(2),−b/sqrt(2),−i)`,

`t=b²`, `T=a0+a2`, `d=−T/t`, `v=d+a2`, `k=d+T`, `rho=2/t`, `beta=t−1`, `gamma=t/(1−t)`.

Then `a0²=a2²=−1`, `a0.a2=1−t`, `d.ai=1`, `d²=−rho`. Define `C=a0.partial X`, `J_B=:C exp(d.X):`, `J_A=:exp(−d.X):`,

`Yi=:exp(ai.(X+Xbar)):`, `K_i=:exp((d+ai).X+ai.Xbar):`,

`U=:exp(v.(X+Xbar)):`, `F_A=:exp(−k.(X+Xbar)):`, `F=:exp(k.(X+Xbar)):`.

The physical cocycle, original constituent labels, inherited boundary heights and auxiliary exponents g,h are exactly those of the preceding common-product report. In particular, g replaces like-current rho powers; h replaces the unlike-source power by `|w0−w2|^(2h−2)`. Restrictions `g=rho,h=t` are taken only after extension, at generic nonresonant t. Let `nu=exp(−i pi/t)` and `s=nu²`.

The finite source-free input currents are `J_B−t lambda2 K2` and `J_A−gamma lambda1 F_A`, and their barred counterparts. The explicit cutoff improvement `+(t/h)lambda0 lambda2 epsilon^(2h)L` is generated by the insertion forest. Equivalently it may be included in the input cutoff representative, but then the identical forest term must not be counted again. The same rule applies to the action counterterm kappa_h.

Let chi be a fixed smooth compact switching function, equal to one on the local region under examination. For nonconstant switching and arbitrary slot tests, all Taylor derivatives are retained. The domain consists of labelled Gaussian Wick clusters, including their distributional coefficients, descendants and source-integrated extensions. A map on formal words alone is not yet a map on the Gaussian quotient: it must send every zero relation of the latter to zero.

## 2. Explicit single-source expansion and forest prescription

Compose the canonical Gaussian cluster G first. Define the candidate through mass order two by

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal I_\chi(G)={}&G
-\sum_{i=0,2}\frac{\lambda_i}{\pi}\int d^2w\,\chi(w)\,
\mathcal R_{\rm bal}W(G,Y_i(w))\\
&-\frac{\lambda_1}{\pi}\int d^2w\,\chi(w)\,
\mathcal R_{\rm bal}W(G,V_1(w))\\
&+\sum_{i\le j\in\{0,2\}}
\frac{\lambda_i\lambda_j}{\pi^2(1+\delta_{ij})}
\int d^2w\,d^2u\,\chi(w)\chi(u)\,
\mathcal R_{\rm bal}W(G,Y_i(w),Y_j(u))+O(m^3).
\end{aligned}
\]

Here W is the actual labelled Gaussian contraction, not an imposed algebra relation. The extension R_bal has the following concrete rules.

1. Retain the two-dimensional Cauchy extension on each single-current/source face. If a spatial-derivative representation is used, add its explicitly calculated unary delta contacts.
2. On a mixed pair/source face use the balanced ordered finite matching calculated in sections 3–4. The phase-aligned upper and lower products receive opposite half corrections. Specify zero additional primitive symmetric local moment at this face; the ordinary principal-value terms are retained.
3. Subtract source-pair and source/composite Taylor jets with their actual radial denominators. The input to each subtraction is the full normal field, spectator contractions, switching functions and slot tests.
4. Combine only disjoint or nested collision faces. Two unary faces consuming the same source at different currents overlap; their delta distributions must never be multiplied. Their union receives the mixed-face extension. Likewise one derivative field can contract to only one source in a Wick matching.
5. Fix no additional primitive local term in a common convergent patch after these proper faces are treated. Continue the complete family jointly in g,h. This is a specified normalization, not a claim that the earlier commutator uniquely fixes all finite products.

The composite-potential counterterm is the source-pair forest representative. It is not a new independent interaction inserted a second time. These rules give an explicit kernel algorithm. Whether that algorithm descends on every relation in a chosen domain is a separate test, addressed below.

## 3. The ordered pair, computed before taking its difference

Place the inner barred B at zero and the outer B at `z=r+i sigma delta`, with `sigma=±1` and `delta=c epsilon`, `c>1`. Align the lower word by its free braiding phase. Write `D=d.partial X`, `A_L=a2.partial X`, and similarly in the right sector. The first normal-field jet of the actual source kernel is obtained from

`exp(D z+A_L w+A_R wbar) [C/(z−w)+beta/(z−w)²] [beta/wbar²−Cbar/wbar]`.

The source circle and the outer Cauchy integral give the ordered moments

`integral sign(sigma delta−Im w)/wbar² = pi sigma`,

`integral_(Im w=sigma delta) dw_real /wbar = i pi sigma`,

`integral_(Im w=sigma delta) dw_real w/wbar² = i pi sigma`.

The limits use a common distant circular infrared cutoff. The coordinator independently integrates its finite-radius horizontal slices and checks both signs. Their combination gives the local raw action term

\[
-i\pi\sigma\nu\lambda_2 t(t-1)\,\partial_xU\,\delta(r).
\]

The first outer-test derivative gives `beta²[pi(i pi sigma)−i pi(pi sigma)]=0` separately in each ordering. Thus this calculation does not conceal a nonzero U delta-prime contact behind a constant outer test.

The first-jet off-diagonal Cauchy convolution also gives

\[
-\nu\lambda_2t(t-1)
\left[\frac{\partial U}{r-i\sigma0}-
\frac{\bar\partial U}{r+i\sigma0}\right].
\]

Its symmetric part is the ordinary dressing

`−nu lambda2 t(t−1)(partial−barpartial)U PV(1/r)`.

Its antisymmetric local part is the delta term above. The balanced finite addition is therefore

`+i pi sigma nu lambda2 t(t−1) partial_x U delta(r)`.

For AA, `(-d).(-T)=2` gives the two scalar double poles, while `−T=−gamma(−k)` converts their first jets to derivatives of F_A. Thus the AA channel has the same structure with `(lambda2,t(t−1),U)` replaced by `(lambda1,−gamma,F_A)`. These ordered half corrections reproduce the previously fixed endpoint differences. Their equal split is an explicit extra symmetric normalization; the earlier difference calculation did not specify it.

The displayed first jets determine the local comparison. They are not the complete nonlocal integral for an arbitrary background; the full W kernel in section 2 supplies the latter.

## 4. The unary contacts that a pair-only prescription misses

The distribution identity is

\[
\operatorname{Pf}\frac1{z^2}
=-\partial_x\frac1z+\pi\delta^{(2)}(z).
\]

It follows from `partial_barz(1/z)=pi delta^(2)(z)` and `partial_x=partial_z+partial_barz`. As an independent check, on the radial test `(1−|z|²)^4` inside the unit disk, the circular finite part is zero, whereas `−partial_x(1/z)` evaluates to `−pi`.

The old outer Cauchy regularization differentiates with partial_z, not partial_x. Both it and the inner circular prescription therefore retain the delta term relative to a spatial derivative construction. Including the action signs and the actual cocycles gives the unary repairs

| Current/source face | Repair relative to spatial differentiation |
|---|---|
| B / Y0 | `+lambda0 K0` |
| B / Y2 | `+(t−1)lambda2 K2` |
| A / V1 | `−lambda1 F_A` |
| Barred counterparts | the exchanged-chirality expressions |

These are terms in the interaction map. They do not redefine the stipulated source-free input tails.

The repair has a measurable symmetric pair part. Put `P=partial U`, `Q=barpartial U`, and suppress nu lambda2. The canonical two tail-current terms have aligned average

\[
2t(t-1)U\operatorname{Pf}\frac1{r^2}
+t[(t-2)P+tQ]\operatorname{PV}\frac1r.
\]

Replacing their coefficient −t by the unary coefficient beta multiplies this by `−beta/t`. Thus the symmetric unary repair is

\[
\boxed{-2(t-1)^2U\operatorname{Pf}\frac1{r^2}
-(t-1)[(t-2)\partial U+t\bar\partial U]\operatorname{PV}\frac1r.}
\]

Its jump is the raw mixed action contact. Balanced matching removes that jump while retaining this symmetric contribution relative to the spatial-derivative source map. The two delta-prime terms cancel. This boxed expression is not an extra term to add again to the already complete rotational integral: it is the conversion between the two extension representations.

There is also a full-field representation before local Taylor truncation, with the common cocycle factored out. For

`G0=1/[(x−w)(y−wbar)]`,

`N=:exp(d.X(x)+d.Xbar(y)+a2.(X(w)+Xbar(wbar))):`,

and `D=d.partial X`, one has

\[
W(B,\bar B,Y_2)=
[C+\beta D-\beta\partial_x]
[\bar C+\beta\bar D-\beta\partial_y](G_0N),
\]

off the source faces. The derivatives act on the entire displayed normal product. The base G0 is locally integrable in the three relative real variables. Its spatial derivatives define a common distribution; the unary and balanced mixed repairs convert it to the selected rotational prescription. The coordinator checks the complete differential polynomial independently, not only its scalar contraction.

## 5. Second-order forests and symmetric descendants

For the unlike pair put `a=w0−w2` and `beta=t−1`. The actual derivative-current rational factor is

\[
f(x;w_0,w_2)=\frac{(x-w_0)^{-1}+\beta(x-w_2)^{-1}}
{(x-w_0)(x-w_2)}.
\]

An exact partial fraction is

\[
\frac1{a(x-w_0)^2}-\frac\beta{a(x-w_2)^2}
+\frac{\beta-1}{a^2}\left[\frac1{x-w_0}-\frac1{x-w_2}\right].
\]

It gives a spatial derivative primitive plus the two correct unary delta terms. A single total derivative with one common double-pole coefficient would be wrong. Source-edge differentiation and this partial-fraction expression give the same separated kernel.

Set w2=0,w0=r. The complete source-pair series is

`f(x;r,0)=sum_(n>=0)(n+t) r^n/x^(n+3)`.

After pairing the two currents and angularly averaging, the nth radial coefficient is

`pi (n+t)² epsilon^(2h+2n)/[(h+n)(z v)^(n+3)]`.

The n=0 term reproduces the potential response. At n=1 the factor is `(t+1)²`, not t²: the derivative descendant has an extra contraction with C. This is an explicit regrouping check with descendants. The coordinator reconstructs modes n=0 through 4 and their radial antiderivatives independently.

Distinct sources may collapse onto distinct currents. The remaining unlike-source factor then has coefficient proportional to

`−beta |x−y|^(2h−4)`.

It vanishes as x approaches y in the physical patch 2<t<3, and its relative one-dimensional degree is 2h−3. It supplies no extra primitive contact there. Two currents consuming the same source are the already prescribed overlapping pair face, not this case.

For endpoint dressing, the U/Y0 kernel is

`|r|^(2h−4)[F+r C F+rbar Cbar F+r rbar C Cbar F+...]`.

Its first two radial denominators are h−1 and h. With a nonconstant smooth coefficient f, the second angular Taylor coefficient is

`f C Cbar + (partial f) Cbar + (barpartial f) C + partial barpartial f`.

Thus the map includes derivative-of-test and switching contacts automatically. Keeping only `C Cbar F` would fail for general tests. With chi=1 locally, the cutoff representatives are

`U −lambda0 epsilon^(2h−2)F/(h−1) −lambda0 epsilon^(2h)C Cbar F/h`,

`K2 −lambda0 epsilon^(2h)L/h`.

Multiplying the latter by the input coefficient −t lambda2 generates the current improvement exactly once. Multiplying the former by the balanced pair coefficient dresses the same matching contact; its Yukawa dressing is not optional.

## 6. Whole-word repair and the first regrouping constraint

A pairwise Jordan product is not an associative multiplication. The map must instead repair the full composed word once. For a word with one barred current, keep the order of its chiral subword P and phase-align the two extreme barred placements. Their half-sum is the balanced candidate. Intermediate placements are related by actual mixed-face crossings; the half corrections telescope to this same value.

For two B currents write aligned raw values R0,R1,R2 and crossings `E2=R0−R1`, `E1=R1−R2`. The common repaired value is `(R0+R2)/2`, with corrections

`C0=−(E1+E2)/2`, `C1=(E2−E1)/2`, `C2=(E1+E2)/2`.

This includes the spectator/test jets in E1,E2. Averaging all three barred positions uniformly gives crossing weights 2/3 and 1/3; it fails the fixed half-matching on the separated proper faces. The two-extreme construction gives one half on each face.

A useful newly required chiral check is the K0 unary insertion. Since `−a0.(d+a0)=0`, the leading scalar in B³A with one B replaced by K0 is

`[−1/(z1−z2)+1/(z1−a)][1/(z1−z2)+1/(z2−a)]+1/(z1−z2)²=0`.

The coordinator and construction worker derive this independently. It removes that candidate marginal scalar. Every higher smooth-field or test jet improves the degree. K0 has the same separated shuffle phases as K2; with K0 replacing the minority B in A³B the full degree is `3(g−rho)+6`. Together with the previously checked K2 and F_A variations, this controls the unary part of the first relation variation.

There is a further analytic step for the actual one-source map. With one source and at most one barred current, its spatially differentiated kernel is a holomorphic directed acyclic graph with at most one antichiral leaf. Orient the edges by the height order. For positive edge momenta k and incidence image q, a discrete topological potential c gives

`sum c_i q_i = sum_(i→j)(c_i−c_j)k_ij >= sum k_ij`.

This gives a proper Fourier pushforward and uniform bounds on smooth test norms independent of all nonnegative height gaps. There are finitely many source-height regions. If the source height stays away from zero, one may take the chiral quartet limit first and use its canonical Serre zero. Regions between the collapsing quartet heights have widths tending to zero; the uniform bound makes their source integrals tend to zero. Spatial differentiation does not differentiate the source height, so it creates no hidden delta there. The rotational delta terms are precisely the unary repairs already retained above.

Consequently the first-order one-source map preserves the tested Serre zero with one barred current. The whole-word extreme-order repair also vanishes on it: both the original Serre expression and its unary variation vanish. This supplies an actual first-order quotient-descent argument, beyond the mixed triple. The adversarial auditor independently checked the graph bound, the source-height limiting step and the K0 variation. It does not settle the two-source forest comparison at mass order two.

## 7. What kind of map this is

This is an operator-valued insertion series. It is linear, contains ordinary action dressing, and in general has `I(1) != 1`. A normalized correlator is a separate construction,

`omega_I(G)=omega_0(I(G))/omega_0(I(1))`,

using a specified Gaussian functional and common infrared treatment. Dividing an operator-valued insertion by an unspecified operator partition function does not define the same object.

Nor should I be required to be multiplicative: `I(GH)` contains one interaction expansion, whereas `I(G)I(H)` counts sources differently. If a well-defined invertible I on a closed completed Gaussian domain is eventually established, one may transport multiplication by

`F star_I G = I(I^(-1)F star I^(-1)G)`.

That formula does not prove the missing domain, quotient descent or source-prescription agreement. It also does not evade the earlier fixed-generator transport obstruction: this interaction map has nontrivial unary corrections and does not fix the generators.
