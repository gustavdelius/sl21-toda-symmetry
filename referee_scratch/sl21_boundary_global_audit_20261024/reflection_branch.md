# Conditional reflection branch check

This supplements the boundary audit. It is an algebraic compatibility check, not a claim that a reflection relation holds in the massive quantum theory.

Put beta=sqrt(-t), B=beta/2, r=-sqrt(2) Phi^u, and A=sqrt(2+beta^2)/2. With b=i beta, direct substitution gives screening exponents B r +/- i A theta and d Phi=r/(2B). Therefore

FA=exp[-(2B+1/(2B))r], U=exp[(B+1/(2B))r-iA theta].

For a wall lambda1 exp(b_L r), b_L=-2B, define Q_L=b_L+1/b_L. The FA momentum is Q_L. If a pure-wall reflection relation V_alpha=R(alpha;lambda1)V_(Q_L-alpha) is available, a constant field translation determines its coupling scaling:

R(alpha;lambda1) proportional to lambda1^((Q_L-2alpha)/b_L).

At alpha=Q_L this exponent is -Q_L/b_L=1/t-1. The action-period cycle has r shift delta=pi i/B. Transforming this cycle to a fixed cycle corresponds to the coupling path lambda1 -> lambda1 exp(-b_L delta), a positive 2 pi winding. The putative reflection coefficient therefore gains exp[2 pi i (1/t-1)]=s^(-1), which agrees with FA's period character.

This resolves a possible apparent contradiction between reflection to the identity within a selected branch and covariance over a period cover. It does not determine the reflection coefficient, justify its evaluation at the identity momentum, establish the corresponding massive-theory reflection identity, or construct a continuum boundary state.
