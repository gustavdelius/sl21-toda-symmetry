# Primary-source audit, 2026-10-24

No novelty claim. This search does not prove absence of a theorem elsewhere. It tests the candidate imports actually used in this investigation. Primary PDFs were read rather than relying on search summaries.

- Evans–Madsen, hep-th/9703065v2, printed pp.8–9, (3.4): the odd-root potential has the explicit factor 1/2 in its ordered double sum. With alpha0.alpha2=1 the two mixed summands give one mu0 mu2 term. Exact source qualification: “the Toda model really does depend on the particular simple root system”. The source image at referee_scratch/sl21_ward_m2_20261023/evans_madsen_eq34.png was inspected. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9703065v2).
- Bernard–LeClair, CMP142 (1991), printed p.106, (2.15)–(2.18): exact qualifiers “We suppose that the same braiding relations hold” and “to lowest non-trivial order in perturbation theory”. Their contour mechanism is usable, but does not construct the derivative-current product or global domain here. The supplied local primary PDF and extracted section 2 were inspected. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02099173).
- Litvinov–Spodyneiko, arXiv:1609.06271, p.9, (4.14)–(4.15): exact line “then it also commutes with the dressed screening field”. Its premise is a W algebra commuting with two fermionic screenings. It supplies the pair-dual derivative screening in a chiral commutant; it does not identify the massive interface-charge algebra. [Source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1609.06271).
- Flandoli–Lentner, SIGMA18 (2022)018, p.2 and Definition3.3: exact qualifier “provided that (m_ij) is subpolar”. The integral collision condition is essential. Our mixed-density products with tails need additional extensions and are not a direct application of the exponential-screening statement. [Source](https://sigma-journal.com/2022/018/sigma22-018.pdf).
- Delius–MacKay, hep-th/0112023v3, section4.1, p.13: “describing an n-component bosonic field” and “For simplicity we shall restrict our attention to simply-laced algebras”. Their source action has ordinary exponential interactions, not this isotropic-root Yukawa action. It does not settle the missing operator matching. This checks relevant work by the editor without importing its symmetry result across actions. [Source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/0112023).
- Fateev, hep-th/0103014v2, printed p.33, (131)–(132): R_s=A_reflected/A_original; shifted momentum a−Q occurs in the coupling exponent as well as the gamma arguments. The actual page was visually inspected. Exact global qualification: “Until now we considered spinless fields and non-compactified field Φ.” The reflection zero/pole was recalculated in intake_checks.py. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0103014v2).
- Fateev, SIGMA13 (2017)080, p.6, (4.1)–(4.3): “For small b one can use the two-dimensional fermion-boson Coleman–Mandelstam correspondence”. This is the first sine-Liouville hierarchy with the negative-exponent wall and a fermionic/cosh/Thirring realization. Normalization converts by Phi_source=sqrt(2) Phi_here, b_source=zeta/sqrt(2), a_source=alpha/sqrt(2), so a_source²−b_source²=1/2 and the kinetic coefficient is 1/(8pi) here. This is local matching, not global bosonization. [Source](https://sigma-journal.com/2017/080/sigma17-080.pdf).
- Lashkevich–Pugai, arXiv:1610.04926, (1.6)–(1.14), printed pp.2–3: “Since we will not discuss here the overall normalization of operators”. Thus normalized form-factor degeneracy cannot replace reflection normalization. Their wall coefficient is −lambda; matching our ordinary-area action requires lambda_LP=−lambda1/pi, not its absolute value. Crossing/completeness is needed for a scalar operator statement. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.04926).

Searches included Delius/sl(2|1)/Toda, sine-Liouville nonlocal symmetry, dressed-screening duality, and the screening/Nichols theorem. Only the primary sources above support conclusions. An unrelated search hit was not used.
