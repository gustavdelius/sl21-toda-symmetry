3. Non-Local Charges in the Sine-Gordon Theory

In this section we describe the non-local charges that characterize the sine-Gordon
theory. We will show that these charges generate the quantum sl(2) loop algebra.
Our analysis provides a new derivation of the soliton S-matrix.
